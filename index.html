Keep It Simple

a better matcha ritual, in four simple steps step by step easy

A simple routine for making matcha part of your everyday.

  1. 1 scoop

    Measure a scoop of vibrant matcha powder into your favourite bowl or cup.

  2. 2 pour

    Add a small splash of hot water, just enough to form a smooth, vivid paste.

  3. 3 whisk

    Whisk gently until the texture turns smooth, light, and softly frothy.

  4. 4 enjoy

    Sip slowly and let the calm, steady energy settle into your day.

Pure by Nature

simple by nature, powerful in every cup. natural whole leaf

From Leaf to Cup

grown slowly under careful shade, kept simple. shade grown stone ground

  • Carefully Grown Under Shade
  • Made From Whole Leaves
  • Slowly Ground Into Powder

Great matcha begins long before it reaches the bowl. Tea leaves are grown under careful shade, helping develop their vibrant colour, smooth character, and naturally rich flavour.

Once harvested, the leaves are gently processed and slowly ground into a fine powder, keeping matcha close to its simplest form: whole, vibrant, and naturally pure.

Shade Grown

Carefully shaded to develop a smoother, richer character.

Carefully Harvested

Young leaves are selected at their freshest and most vibrant.

Slowly Stone Ground

Gently milled for a fine powder, smooth, and silky texture.

Nothing Unnecessary

Pure matcha with no added flavours, colours, or extras.
